Skarabe — privacy and personal data

Pre-release review text — not yet effective. This notice covers the planned invited 16+ beta. It will not be published as final before the international transfer arrangements and proposed legal grounds below have been reviewed. Optional measurements are disabled. Version: 31 October 2026.

1. Controller and scope

Mustafa Dağ is the publisher and controller for game-related processing in the planned beta. Support and data requests: support@skarabe.io. No game account is required. This notice covers the game, beta access and support. Other services we link to also process data under their own terms.

2. Intended age

The beta is for invited players aged 16 and over. Entry requires a 16+ declaration, not a date of birth or identity document. This is not an official game age rating or age verification. Do not join if you are under 16. If participation contrary to this age policy is discovered, access and related records will be reviewed.

4. Review of legal grounds

The proposed pre-release distinction is: processing necessary for the beta/online game service you request under KVKK Article 5(2)(c), establishment or performance of a contract; necessary and proportionate abuse prevention under legitimate interests balanced against your rights, Article 5(2)(f); and statutory requests or required records under an applicable legal obligation, Article 5(2)(ç), or establishment, exercise or protection of rights, Article 5(2)(e). The final review must confirm these grounds against the actual service and records. This paragraph does not establish international transfer safeguards.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local progress and preferences remain until you or your browser clear site data. Clearing them also removes progress, but not server records.
  • The beta session cookie lasts up to eight hours. A server restart ends the session. Expired or revoked invitations cannot authorize access. Login-limiter IP records remain in server memory for up to one minute.
  • Invitation hashes, expiry and revocation status are held in a file to manage access. Operator removal within 30 days after expiry/revocation is planned; the invitation tool does not automate this cleanup.
  • Online leaderboard records use an eight-day retention window and are deleted at the next hourly cleanup on a running server. Temporary game state is removed from memory through the room/connection lifecycle.
  • The operator deletes support correspondence within 90 days after the request closes. Records necessary for a legal retention obligation or ongoing dispute may be retained with a recorded reason and end date. This is not an indefinite-retention exception. Providers apply their own periods to backups and security records.

6. Providers and international processing

The game server is configured for Render's Frankfurt, Germany region. This does not mean all provider support or subprocessor activity stays in Germany. The skarabe.io static site uses GitHub Pages; GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security. A beta page served by Render does not pass through GitHub Pages; opening the Pages site invokes GitHub's processing.

Email to support@skarabe.io is forwarded by Namecheap to skarabe.support@gmail.com. These providers may process your message and email information; replies may come from Gmail. The services may involve processing or access outside Türkiye.

7. Measurements, advertising and sharing

This beta does not start game analytics, performance telemetry or Cloudflare Web Analytics, and optional measurement permission cannot be enabled. Necessary game/network traffic is separate. Any later activation requires updated scope, retention and preference information first. Legacy telemetry files are pruned against a 90-day window at startup and daily; day-based files and cleanup intervals may delay deletion. This does not mean the new beta collects measurements.

Portal advertising SDKs are not loaded in invited beta mode. Twitch integration is not enabled in this game configuration. Clips are prepared on your device; choosing export/sharing sends the file or link to the service you select. That service may then process information under its own terms.

8. Rights and requests

Subject to KVKK's conditions, you have rights to learn whether data is processed, obtain information, learn the purpose, use and recipients, correct inaccurate/incomplete data, request deletion/destruction and notification to recipients when the conditions apply, object to an adverse result based solely on automated analysis, and seek compensation for unlawful processing. Additional rights under other applicable data protection law and routes to the competent supervisory authority remain available where relevant.

For initial contact and requests, email support@skarabe.io with a subject and explanation. Include your player/measurement ID privately if relevant; do not post it in a public comment. Proportionate verification may be needed. KVKK's request procedures and mandatory information requirements remain applicable; ordinary support email is not claimed to replace every formal request method. A published registered-email or postal request address has not yet been designated.

KVKK requests are handled as soon as their nature permits and within 30 days; any different period under other applicable law is respected. KVKK complaint routes remain available where a request is rejected, answered inadequately or not answered in time.

9. Changes

This text is for the planned beta and does not indicate that the live site has been updated. Material changes to data flows or providers will be reflected before the related processing starts. A privacy notice is not a measurement-consent or service-terms acceptance button.
